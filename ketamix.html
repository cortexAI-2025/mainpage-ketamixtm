What Is KETAMIX

Between Cultivation
and Formulation

KETAMIX operates at the critical interface between raw botanical cultivation and advanced formulation. It does not simply supply biomass — it transforms, classifies, and standardizes it into structured, extract-ready intermediates designed for sophisticated industrial use.

The platform logic is founded on a principle of multi-ratio cannabinoid architecture: each product family is defined by a deliberate, documented profile — not by harvest chance. Every batch reflects the same industrial discipline, independent of season, volume, or supplier.

KETAMIX exists to give B2B processors, formulation developers, and regulated supply chain operators a single, credible, Moroccan-origin botanical input source with the consistency and documentation standards they require.

Technical Philosophy

Specifications
Built for
Industrial Use

KETAMIX applies a structured technical methodology across every stage of biomass processing — from initial field classification through to extract-ready lot release. The approach is designed to eliminate input variability at the processor level.

Documentation is not a post-process addition. It is built into the classification workflow from the first grading step.

COA-Oriented Workflow Standard
Biomass Classification Multi-tier grading protocol applied at intake. Visual, dimensional, and cannabinoid-profile classification determines family assignment before processing begins.
Moisture Control Controlled drying and moisture stabilization protocols applied to all lots. Moisture content validated against extract-ready suitability thresholds before batch release.
Cannabinoid Profiling Each batch is analytically profiled to confirm cannabinoid ratio classification. Profiles are validated against family tolerances and documented in batch records.
Pre-Extraction Suitability Lot preparation accounts for downstream extraction method compatibility — CO₂, ethanol, hydrocarbon. Particle preparation and contamination screening applied accordingly.
Batch Documentation Each classified lot ships with a structured technical record including batch ID, origin data, cannabinoid profile summary, grading outcome, and moisture report.
Traceability Integration Batch IDs are structured for integration with QR-coded logistics, digital supply chain platforms, and regulatory documentation workflows where applicable.
COA-Ready Workflow Internal processes are structured to support third-party COA issuance. KETAMIX documentation format is designed to align with regulated market compliance expectations.
Moroccan Origin

Rif Heritage.
Industrial
Discipline.

Morocco carries one of the world's most historically significant cannabis cultivation traditions — concentrated in the Rif region, where unique climatic, altitudinal, and soil conditions have shaped distinct botanical genetics across generations.

KETAMIX positions this origin not as a folkloric reference, but as a traceable botanical asset. Moroccan cultivation provides a genuine competitive distinction — provable, documentable, and increasingly valued within regulated ingredient markets.

The Moroccan regulatory transition represents a structural opportunity to redirect this agricultural heritage toward standardized, value-added, internationally compliant botanical supply chains — replacing raw commodity logic with industrial input intelligence.
