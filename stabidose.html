Pharmacological Basis

The Science Behind STABIDOSE

Cannabinoid pharmacology is fundamentally multi-target in nature. STABIDOSE formulations are designed around this biological reality — not isolated molecules, but reproducible multi-ratio profiles engaging complementary receptor systems.

🔬

Nav1.8 Ion Channel Modulation

CBG has demonstrated strong inhibitory activity at Nav1.8 voltage-gated sodium channels, which play a critical role in peripheral pain signaling and neuronal excitability. This mechanism positions CBG-enriched formulations as a potential non-opioid analgesic pathway, distinct from classical CB1-mediated effects.

Nav1.8 CBG Pain modulation Non-opioid
🧬

Multi-Target Receptor Engagement

Cannabinoids simultaneously interact with a broad receptor landscape including endocannabinoid, serotonergic, vanilloid, and inflammatory pathways. This polypharmacological profile underpins STABIDOSE's rationale for multi-ratio botanical standardization over single-molecule approaches.

CB1 / CB2 TRPV1 5-HT1A COX-2 FAAH Adenosine
⚗️

Full-Spectrum vs. Isolate: Emerging Evidence

A growing body of preclinical and observational evidence suggests that carefully standardized botanical cannabinoid extracts may offer superior therapeutic profiles compared to isolated single molecules. This entourage-like effect is dependent on consistent cannabinoid and terpene ratios — which STABIDOSE achieves through rigorous botanical standardization within ±5% tolerance per batch.

Botanical extract ±5% tolerance Entourage evidence
🌿

Acid Cannabinoid Preservation (CBDa / CBGa)

Acid-form cannabinoids (CBDa, CBGa) represent a pharmacologically relevant frontier. STABIDOSE incorporates a validated SOP for acid-preserving extraction using selected CBDa/CBGa-dominant mother plants, enabling whole-plant acid-form formulations for emerging research applications in inflammation and nausea pathways.

Formulation Architecture

The Multi-Ratio Approach

The STABIDOSE platform is built on the principle that therapeutic outcomes are determined not only by which cannabinoids are present, but by their precise relative proportions — and that reproducibility of those proportions from batch to batch is the defining quality criterion.

Standard cannabinoid preparations often lack inter-batch consistency. STABIDOSE addresses this through a dual-layer biomass strategy: industrial-stability genetics providing a reproducible cannabinoid baseline, overlaid with Moroccan Beldiya terroir fractions for full-spectrum terpene authenticity. Each formulation target is defined by a precise ratio architecture — not a single dominant molecule.

KETAMIX™, the upstream standardized botanical blend feedstock, ensures that every STABIDOSE formulation starts from a chemically defined, fully traceable input. Ratio deviations are contained within ±5% through validated blending protocols and systematic COA verification.

This architecture enables scalable pharmaceutical manufacturing while preserving the biological complexity of full-spectrum botanical extracts — a standard not achievable with isolate-based approaches.

Key principle: A formulation's therapeutic reproducibility is only as reliable as the reproducibility of its cannabinoid input. KETAMIX™ → STABIDOSE™ is a vertically integrated standardization chain, from cultivar to capsule.

Clinical Research

Clinical Development Program

STABIDOSE is actively developing structured clinical evidence to support both regulatory submissions and scientific publication. The first investigator-initiated pilot study targets chronic pain and opioid-sparing endpoints at CHU Ibn Rochd, Casablanca.

Study Design CHU Casablanca

Prospective observational pilot study (Phase 0 / IIT). 40–80 patients, 8–12 week follow-up. Multi-arm design (2–3 arms subject to ethics committee approval).

  • Chronic musculo-skeletal or neuropathic pain cohort
  • Oncological pain / palliative care cohort
  • Sleep disorder + pain-associated anxiety cohort
  • Opioid/benzodiazepine reduction sub-group

Outcome Measures Validated Scales

International standard psychometric instruments are designated as primary and secondary endpoints, enabling direct comparison with existing cannabinoid clinical literature.

  • Brief Pain Inventory (BPI) — pain severity + interference
  • Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (PSQI) / ISI
  • GAD-7 — anxiety severity scale
  • SF-12 / EQ-5D — quality of life
  • MME — morphine milligram equivalent reduction
  • ALAT/ASAT, bilirubin — hepatic safety monitoring

Product Standard Study-Grade

Each clinical batch is documented to study-grade pharmaceutical standard, enabling regulatory-quality evidence generation and future dossier submission.

  • Full cannabinoid COA per clinical batch
  • Contaminant panel (pesticides, metals, mycotoxins, microbio)
  • Stability documentation per study period
  • Sealed, QR-traced individual packaging
  • Pre-GMP / ISO 9001 + ISO 14644 manufacturing
